Research Skills: Find and Evaluate Information

Effective research is fundamental to academic success. Strong research skills help you find reliable information, evaluate sources critically, and synthesize knowledge.

Developing a Research Question

A focused research question guides your entire project. Start broad, then narrow down. Use the PICO framework for scientific questions or the 5 Ws for humanities. Your question should be specific, researchable, and significant.

Search Strategies and Databases

Learn advanced search techniques using Boolean operators. Use academic databases like Google Scholar, PubMed, JSTOR, and Scopus. Reference lists of relevant papers lead to additional sources. Organize searches and document your process.

Evaluating Source Credibility

Consider the CRAAP test: Currency, Relevance, Authority, Accuracy, and Purpose. Check publication dates, author credentials, publisher reputation, and cited sources. Peer-reviewed journals are generally most reliable. Be wary of predatory journals.

Conducting a Literature Review

A literature review surveys existing research on your topic. It identifies gaps, debates, and established findings. Organize sources thematically or chronologically. Synthesize rather than summarize, showing relationships between different works.

Organizing Research Findings

Use reference managers like Zotero or Mendeley to collect and organize sources. Create an annotated bibliography summarizing each source's relevance. Use digital note-taking systems to connect ideas across sources. Develop a filing system that works for you.
