About me / 站主简介

I'm Tan Haosheng (谭好升) — a thyroid and breast surgeon based in Taizhou, China. My day job is clinical care and research, and outside it I run AI Tool Stack, an independent, source-backed guide to AI tools that actually help in clinical and research workflows.

I came to AI the way most doctors do: skeptical, time-poor, and allergic to hype. What kept me using it wasn't the demos — it was the quiet wins: a half-decent draft of a discharge summary at 11pm, a model that could compare two guidelines side-by-side without me retyping, a free-tier chatbot that handled 80% of the English polish on a manuscript. So this site is a record of what actually works for me, with prices, where I tested, and where I didn't.

I lean toward cost-effective models — partly because hospital budgets don't stretch to GPT-4-class subscriptions, partly because I want to know which of these tools are still useful when you're not paying top dollar. If a cheap model fails a task a human can do in 30 seconds, I want to know that before a patient does.

Why I write this site

  • Honesty first. Every page states what I tested hands-on vs. what comes from the vendor or third parties. I never claim testing I did not perform.
  • Cited, verifiable data. Performance numbers, pricing and specs link back to their original source.
  • Cost-conscious. For most clinical / research tasks I run, a $0–$20/month model is enough. I want to know exactly which one.
  • Independent. Some pages may include affiliate links, but reviews reflect my own assessment. Corrections welcome by email.
  • For fellow surgeons. My goal is to help surgeons use AI tools better and more cost-effectively — clear, source-backed, hands-on, with no hype.
  • Now testing AI agents, not just models. AI Agents for Medical Research Writing is the new sibling section: real case studies of an AI agent evaluating my own single-cell data and manuscripts, with full reproducible transcripts.
