Claude 3 Opus — Clinical LLM Test: Breast Cancer Case

Anthropic's flagship model tested on a post-operative breast cancer case. Results coming soon.

🚧 Coming Soon — Claude 3 Opus clinical benchmark data is being collected. This page will be updated with full results.

Why Claude 3 Opus?

Claude 3 Opus is Anthropic's most capable model for complex reasoning tasks. We're testing it on the same breast cancer case used across our clinical LLM benchmark series.

Test Methodology

  • Case: Post-operative breast cancer (60F, pT1aN0M0 IA)
  • Input: De-identified outpatient case screenshot
  • Scoring: 16-item rubric vs 2026 CBCS guidelines (max 100)
  • Runs: 20 iterations to measure stability
