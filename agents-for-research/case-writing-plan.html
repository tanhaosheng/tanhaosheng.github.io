Case 3 — From raw matrix to a publication plan

By Tan Haosheng, MD, PhD · Last reviewed 2026-07-31

Forward-lookingOne promptWorkBuddy Hy31 patient · 2 samples5 routes + journal targets

Same two de-identified breast-cancer samples, same single patient. This time I did not ask "which one was sorted" and I did not ask "does my manuscript hold." I asked the agent to read the raw matrix and tell me, as a researcher who has to publish, what could actually be written from this, and where would it go. That is the capability Cases 1 and 2 never exercised: looking forward, not just catching mistakes.

⚠️ About the numbers and figures on this page

The single-cell dataset behind this case supports a manuscript that has not been submitted yet. I am not free to publish the underlying values, and I am not going to pretend otherwise.

So: every figure below was redrawn from the real analysis with the values deliberately perturbed, and the numbers quoted in the text are rounded to low precision. What is preserved is the direction, ranking and rough magnitude of each effect — which is the part that matters for judging what the AI agent did. What is not preserved is any exact value.

Read the charts as illustrations of a pattern, not as data. Once the manuscript is published I will swap in the unmodified figures and link the real tables.

The prompt I gave the agent (verbatim)

这两个乳腺癌单细胞样本（一个 CD45 分选、一个未分选，同一患者）的原始数据在 /Users/xx/Desktop/SCI/单细胞测序/results 下面。请你做一份完整的分析，重点告诉我：这份数据本身能支撑写什么文章？如果要做，往哪些期刊投比较现实？可行性怎么样？请诚实评估，不要为了迎合我而夸大。

Again, plain language. No scaffold, no "list five bullet points" instruction. The one constraint I added — "don't inflate it to please me" — is the whole point of this case.

Result 1 — The agent's read of the tumour's identity

Before proposing anything, the agent reconstructed the tumour subtype from the data. Its conclusion: an HER2-driven, hormone-receptor-negative breast cancer, with the HER2 amplification visible directly at single-cell resolution.

SignalAgent's read
CNVA focal 17q12 spike in tumour cells only — the ERBB2 amplicon shows up as one narrow peak above a flat genome, while CAF and immune reference stay at zero.
Hormone axisESR1 and PGR essentially silent across epithelial clusters — read as ER/PR-negative, i.e. HR⁻.
Co-amplificationThe 17q12 amplicon genes travel together, while other chr17 loci do not — consistent with focal, not whole-arm, gain.
ProliferationLow MKI67/TOP2A — a non-proliferating phenotype, which the agent flagged as possibly chemo-resistant and targeted-therapy-responsive (hedged, not asserted).

This is the "reverse" of Case 1. There, the agent checked my claims against data. Here, it generated the conclusion from data with no claim handed to it. Same rigor, opposite direction.

Result 2 — The microenvironment it proposed writing about

The agent's headline finding for the TME: a cold, immune-excluded phenotype. The suppressive and barrier programmes are loud; antigen presentation and interferon are silent. It organized the evidence into a single phenotype chart.

✓ The four pillars it pulled out

  • myCAF barrier, iCAF absent. COL11A1 / POSTN / LRRC15 high; the classic recruiting iCAF genes (CXCL12, IL6) near zero. A physical-plus-biochemical wall.
  • SPP1⁺ TAM dominant. The macrophage programme scores strongly positive and, the agent noted, is the kind of stromal signature repeatedly tied to immunotherapy resistance in 2023–2024 Nature/Cell papers.
  • Dendritic cells essentially missing. The cDC signature is at the floor — which the agent read as "naive T-cell priming has no stage to happen on."
  • Treg, not exhaustion. Roughly a quarter of T cells are Treg; classic exhaustion markers (PDCD1, LAG3, HAVCR2) are near zero. Suppression, not exhaustion, is the dominant mode.

Result 3 — The method twist it refused to waste

The agent noticed something Cases 1–2 used but never framed as a paper: the CD45 sort is a built-in environmental-RNA cleaner, and it demonstrably does not distort the immune landscape.

Method claimEvidence the agent cited
CD45 sort removes ambient RNAEpithelial-gene positivity inside immune cells drops from near-universal to roughly a fifth-to-half after sorting.
CD45 sort does not bias the immune landscapeImmune composition correlates almost perfectly (rank correlation > 0.96) between the sorted and unsorted samples.

Result 4 — The five write-ups and where they'd go

This is the part I actually asked for. The agent returned a table of five routes with feasibility and target journals — and a hard honesty statement up front.

The constraint it led with

One patient, two samples can only sustain a Letter / Brief Report / Case Report / Methods Note. Anything pan-cohort needs more samples. The agent refused to dress this up as more than it is — exactly the instruction I gave it, and the behaviour I was testing.

D. Feature: myCAF barrier vs T-cell trap"LRRC15⁺ myCAF walls off T cells"Needs spatial / mIF validationCancer Discov / Cell Reports Med (IF 10+)

The agent's recommended path was D + C combined: add spatial transcriptomics and multiplex immunofluorescence to the same sample to nail the myCAF barrier, then merge 2–3 public HER2⁺ datasets for a small-cohort integrative analysis. It named a concrete candidate title and a realistic journal ladder (a 3–4 IF entry, up to a 7–10 IF option). It also gave the low-cost version: skip new experiments, go straight to route C, draft in 1–2 weeks.

What this case taught me about AI agents

  • Forward generation is a separate skill from contradiction-hunting. An agent that catches your errors (Case 1) is not automatically one that can originate a research plan. This case tested the second, and it held.
  • The honesty instruction actually worked. I told it not to flatter me, and it led with the n=1 ceiling instead of burying it. The cheapest way to get a useful plan is to explicitly forbid the agent from pleasing you.
  • Journal targeting from real impact factors beats "submit to a good journal." Concrete tiers (4–6, 5–14, 10+) turned a vague ambition into a ladder I can actually climb.
  • A method footnote can be a paper. The CD45-sort-as-cleaner observation (Result 3) was the lowest-effort, lowest-risk route — and the agent surfaced it without prompting.

Limitations of this case

  • One patient, two samples. Every biological claim is "this case," not "breast cancer."
  • Platform and pipeline are single-vendor; signatures are only as good as the annotation.
  • DC absence and the myCAF barrier need orthogonal validation (flow, spatial) before any strong claim.
  • I cannot publish the underlying values here (manuscript unpublished); figures are perturbed illustrations by policy.
