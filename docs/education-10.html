Learning on YouTube: Educational Channels You Need

YouTube is not just for entertainment it is one of the largest free educational platforms in the world. Quality channels offer university-level content accessible to anyone.

Science and Technology Channels

Kurzgesagt explains complex topics with stunning animations. Veritasium explores scientific principles through experiments. 3Blue1Brown makes higher mathematics intuitive through visual explanations. Computerphile covers computer science fundamentals.

History and Humanities

Crash Course offers fast-paced overviews of world history, literature, and science. Oversimplified breaks down historical events with humor. The School of Life explores philosophy and emotional intelligence. Extra Credits covers history through storytelling.

Study Tips and Productivity

Thomas Frank and Ali Abdaal provide evidence-based study techniques and productivity systems. Better Ideas explores personal development. These channels help you learn how to learn more effectively.

Creating an Effective YouTube Learning Playlist

Organize videos into topic-based playlists. Watch at 1.25x or 1.5x speed for efficiency. Take notes and pause to reflect. Supplement videos with reading and practice. Avoid the recommendation rabbit hole by focusing on your curated content.

Verifying Educational Quality

Check the creator's credentials and cited sources. Peer-reviewed content is more reliable. Cross-reference important facts. Some channels are produced in collaboration with universities and offer genuine educational value.
