Kimi 3 AI: Complete Guide

China's long-context AI assistant by Moonshot AI. 200K+ context window, file processing, web search — and it's free.

Kimi, developed by Beijing-based Moonshot AI (月之暗面), has become one of China's most popular AI assistants. Its claim to fame? An enormous context window that lets it process entire books, research papers, and massive codebases in one go. By 2026, Kimi 3 has established itself as a serious competitor to DeepSeek, Qwen, and even GPT-4o for long-document tasks.

What is Kimi 3?

Kimi is a conversational AI assistant built for handling massive amounts of text. While most AI models handle 8K-200K tokens of context, Kimi was designed from the ground up for long-form content. Its name comes from the Chinese word for "crisp" or "clean" — reflecting its focus on clear, structured responses.

Key milestones: Kimi launched in late 2023, gained millions of users in China by 2024, and Kimi 3 (released mid-2026) brought significant improvements in reasoning, coding, and multilingual capabilities.

How to Use Kimi 3

Accessing Kimi

Visit the website or download the app

Go to kimi.moonshot.cn on your browser, or search "Kimi AI" in your app store. The mobile app is available for both iOS and Android.

Sign up with phone or email

Kimi supports Chinese phone numbers, email registration, and WeChat login. International users may need a Chinese phone number for full access.

Start chatting or upload files

Type your questions directly, or click the upload button to attach files. Kimi accepts PDF, Word, Excel, PPT, TXT, and image files. It reads the text content and responds based on it.

Use web search for real-time info

Toggle the web search button (🌐 icon) to let Kimi search the internet. This is great for news, current events, and fact-checking.

What Kimi 3 Excels At

Long-document analysis

Upload a 300-page research report and ask Kimi to summarize it, extract key findings, or compare chapters. Its massive context window means no information is lost.

Academic research

Load multiple papers, have Kimi compare methodologies, identify research gaps, or generate literature reviews. This is where Kimi truly outshines most competitors.

Contract and legal document review

Upload contracts in PDF or Word format. Kimi can identify clauses, flag risks, and summarize terms across dozens of pages.

Coding with full codebase awareness

Paste entire code files or repositories. Kimi understands the full context and gives more accurate code suggestions and bug fixes.

Who Should Use Kimi 3?

Kimi is particularly well-suited for:

  • Researchers and academics — processing papers, extracting findings, literature reviews
  • Lawyers and legal professionals — contract review, clause analysis, case research
  • Students — studying from textbooks, summarizing course materials
  • Content creators — researching topics from multiple sources
  • Anyone who works with large documents — reports, manuals, technical documentation

Limitations

  • Primarily optimized for Chinese language (English is good but not its strongest)
  • Image generation not available
  • Voice and multimodal capabilities behind GPT-4o
  • International access requires Chinese phone number for full features
  • Smaller plugin/ecosystem compared to ChatGPT

FAQ

Is Kimi 3 free?

Yes, Kimi has a very generous free tier that covers most everyday needs. There's also a paid Pro version with higher usage limits and faster response times. Compared to ChatGPT Plus ($20/month), Kimi's pricing is very affordable.

Can I use Kimi from outside China?

Kimi's web app (kimi.moonshot.cn) can be accessed internationally, but the mobile app is primarily available on Chinese app stores. For full features including phone registration, a Chinese phone number is helpful but not strictly required for basic web access.

How does Kimi compare to DeepSeek?

DeepSeek is stronger at reasoning, math, and coding. Kimi excels at long-document processing and file-based tasks. They complement each other — use DeepSeek for technical work and Kimi for document analysis.

Can Kimi process images?

Kimi can read text from images (OCR), but it cannot generate images or analyze visual content the way GPT-4o or Gemini can. It's primarily a text-focused model with strong document understanding.

Does Kimi have an API?

Yes, Moonshot AI offers API access for developers. Pricing is competitive with other Chinese AI providers, positioning between DeepSeek (cheapest) and Qwen (balanced).
