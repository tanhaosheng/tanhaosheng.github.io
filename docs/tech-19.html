VPN Guide: How to Choose and Use a VPN

A Virtual Private Network (VPN) creates an encrypted tunnel between your device and a remote server, protecting your internet traffic from prying eyes. VPNs are essential tools for online privacy, security on public WiFi, accessing geo-restricted content, and maintaining anonymity online.

How VPNs Work

When you connect to a VPN, your internet traffic is encrypted and routed through a server operated by the VPN provider. Your real IP address is hidden, and the websites you visit see the VPN server's IP instead. This prevents your internet service provider, government agencies, and malicious actors from monitoring your online activities.

VPN Protocols Compared

WireGuard is the modern standard: it is faster, more secure, and simpler than older protocols. OpenVPN remains widely used and highly configurable. IKEv2/IPsec offers good speed and stability, particularly on mobile devices. Older protocols like PPTP and L2TP should be avoided due to known security vulnerabilities. Most premium VPNs now default to WireGuard.

Choosing a VPN Provider

When selecting a VPN service, consider the privacy policy (look for a verified no-logs policy), server network size and locations, connection speed, simultaneous device connections, platform support, and price. Independent security audits provide assurance that the provider delivers on its privacy promises. Avoid free VPNs, as they often monetize by selling user data.

What VPNs Cannot Do

VPNs do not make you completely anonymous online. Cookies, browser fingerprinting, and account logins can still identify you. VPNs do not protect against malware, phishing, or social engineering attacks. Using a VPN does not give you immunity for illegal activities. A VPN is one tool in a broader security and privacy strategy.

Setting Up a VPN

Most VPN services offer user-friendly apps for Windows, macOS, iOS, and Android that require just a login to activate. For routers, you can install VPN firmware for whole-home protection. Advanced users can set up their own VPN server using WireGuard on a VPS for maximum control and privacy.

A quality VPN is an essential tool for online privacy and security. Choose a reputable provider with a verified no-logs policy, use modern protocols like WireGuard, and understand the limitations of VPN technology for a comprehensive approach to online safety.
