Khan Academy: Free Education for Everyone

Khan Academy is a nonprofit educational platform offering free, world-class instruction in subjects from arithmetic to art history. It democratizes learning for anyone, anywhere.

Comprehensive Subject Coverage

Math covers early arithmetic through college-level calculus and linear algebra. Science includes physics, chemistry, biology, and electrical engineering. Humanities cover history, economics, art history, and civics. Test prep includes SAT, LSAT, and MCAT.

Personalized Learning Dashboard

The platform adapts to your level. Mastery learning exercises let you progress at your own pace. The dashboard tracks your progress, identifies gaps, and recommends practice. This personalized approach ensures you build strong foundations.

How Teachers and Parents Use Khan Academy

Teachers assign lessons and track class progress. Parents can create coach accounts to monitor their children's learning. The platform aligns with common core standards and is widely used in school districts for supplemental instruction.

Effective Learning Strategies on Khan Academy

Watch videos at your own pace, pausing to take notes. Complete practice problems until you reach mastery level. Use hints strategically when stuck. Revisit previously mastered topics periodically to maintain proficiency.

The Impact of Free Education

Khan Academy has served hundreds of millions of learners worldwide. Its mission is particularly impactful for underserved communities lacking access to quality educational resources. The platform continues to expand with new content and features.
