edX: Access Top University Courses Online

Founded by Harvard and MIT, edX provides access to high-quality courses from the world's leading universities. It is a powerful platform for academic and professional growth.

Course Formats and Credentials

edX offers verified certificates for a fee, MicroMasters programs that count toward graduate degrees, Professional Certificate programs, and fully online master's degrees. Audit tracks are available free for most courses.

Popular Subject Areas

Computer science, data science, business management, engineering, humanities, and public health are among the most popular categories. Courses from Harvard's CS50 series and MIT's supply chain management program are consistently top-rated.

Learning on edX Effectively

Courses include video lectures, interactive exercises, discussion forums, and graded assignments. Set aside dedicated time each week. Engage with the community through forums. Complete all assignments to reinforce learning.

Financial Assistance Options

edX provides financial assistance for verified certificates and programs. The application considers your circumstances and learning goals. Some courses also offer free certificates through specific programs or institutional partnerships.

Pathway to Academic Credit

MicroMasters programs can count toward a full master's degree at partner universities. Credit-eligible courses provide official transcripts. This pathway makes graduate education more accessible and affordable.
