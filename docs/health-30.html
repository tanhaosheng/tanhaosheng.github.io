Eye Care: Protecting Your Vision for Life

Vision is one of our most precious senses. With simple preventive measures and regular care, you can maintain healthy eyesight throughout your life.

The 20-20-20 Rule for Screen Time

Digital eye strain causes dry eyes, headaches, and blurred vision. Follow the 20-20-20 rule: every 20 minutes, look at something 20 feet away for 20 seconds. This relaxes the focusing muscles and reduces fatigue from prolonged screen use.

Nutrition for Eye Health

Lutein and zeaxanthin, found in leafy greens, protect against macular degeneration. Omega-3 fatty acids support tear production and reduce dry eye. Vitamins A, C, and E, along with zinc, are essential for maintaining healthy vision as you age.

UV Protection for Your Eyes

Ultraviolet radiation damages eyes over time, contributing to cataracts and macular degeneration. Wear sunglasses that block 99 to 100 percent of UVA and UVB rays whenever you are outdoors, even on cloudy days. Wide-brimmed hats add extra protection.

Regular Eye Exams Matter

Comprehensive eye exams detect not just vision problems but also signs of systemic conditions like diabetes, high blood pressure, and autoimmune diseases. Adults should have an eye exam every one to two years, or more frequently if they wear corrective lenses.

Common Eye Conditions

Nearsightedness, farsightedness, and astigmatism are correctable with glasses or contacts. Cataracts, glaucoma, and age-related macular degeneration become more common with age. Early detection through regular exams provides the best treatment outcomes.
