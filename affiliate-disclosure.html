Affiliate Disclosure

Short version: AI Tool Stack is an independent guide. Some pages on this site may contain affiliate (referral) or promotional links. If you sign up or subscribe through such a link, I may earn a commission — but it never increases your price and never changes my editorial conclusions. I only recommend tools I have tested hands-on.

What is an affiliate link?

An affiliate (or referral) link contains a unique identifier that tells the vendor "this visitor came from AI Tool Stack." If that visitor later pays for a plan, the vendor shares a portion of that revenue with the site as a referral fee. This is a standard, disclosure-based way for independent review sites to fund their work.

Does it affect you?

  • Your price is unchanged. Affiliate commissions are paid by the vendor out of their own margin; the listed price is the same whether you use my link or navigate directly.
  • My reviews stay independent. Every tool on this site is evaluated against the same hands-on criteria (real tasks, real screenshots, real numbers). A vendor cannot buy a better score, and I never claim testing I did not perform.
  • You can always bypass the link. If you prefer, simply open the vendor's site in a new tab and sign up there — you lose nothing.

How we mark promotional content

When a link or call-to-action on this site is promotional, we label it clearly — for example with a "🚧 推广链接申请中 / Affiliate link pending" tag or a visible "Advertisement / 广告" marker. Where no such marker appears, the link is either editorial (non-affiliate) or an internal reference to our own content.
